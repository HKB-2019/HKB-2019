**Every time:**
- Ask for the largest size and download the full file, never a screenshot.
- Never use "flawless", "perfect skin", "smooth" or "beautiful lighting": they cause the plastic look.
- Don't name real models or celebrities: ChatGPT may refuse, and the faces must be MASQ.'s own.

**Quick fixes, as a reply in the same chat:**
- Plastic: *"Keep their faces and beauty exactly, but make it a real photograph: visible pores, fine hairs, natural skin texture, as if shot on film and not retouched. Less contrast and less sharpening on the faces."*
- Face too thin: *"Too lean: fuller, healthier cheeks with no hollows, a little more width in the jaw. Keep everything else."*
- Face changed: *"That is not Dayo (or Ifeoma). Keep the face exactly as in their test shot."*
- 4 doesn't line up with 3: *"Same framing as Dayo's top-of-page photo: same size in the frame, same place on the right, same height, same wall and light."*

These are AI-made models: on the site they are campaign photos, never shown as real customers or reviews.

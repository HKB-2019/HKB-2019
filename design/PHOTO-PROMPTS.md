# Prompts for new model photos

For ChatGPT. **Seven messages, all in the same chat, in order.** Messages 1 and 2 create the two models (test shots only); 3 to 7 are the photos for the site. Each photo is asked for on its own, never as a whole page design, so the models fill the picture and faces and prints keep their detail.

| # | Makes | Attach |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tobi, the male model (test shot) | the MASQ. logo |
| 2 | Amara, the female model (test shot) | nothing |
| 3 | Top of the home page: both together | the logo and the old home-page image |
| 4 | Countdown: Amara in the patterned hood | nothing |
| 5 | Violet T-shirt product photo: Tobi | the logo |
| 6 | Black T-shirt product photo: Amara | the logo |
| 7 | Hoodie product photo: Tobi | the logo |

**Every time:**
- Wait for each picture before sending the next message.
- Ask for the largest size and download the full file, never a screenshot.
- Never use "flawless", "perfect skin", "smooth" or "beautiful lighting": they cause the plastic look.
- Don't name real models or celebrities: ChatGPT may refuse, and the faces must be MASQ.'s own.

**Quick fixes, as a reply in the same chat:**
- Plastic: *"More real: visible pores, fine hairs and natural texture, as if shot on film and not retouched. Less contrast on the face."*
- Face too thin: *"Too lean: fuller, healthier cheeks with no hollows, a little more width in the jaw. Keep everything else."*
- Face changed: *"That is not Tobi (or Amara). Keep the face exactly as in their test shot."*

These are AI-made models: on the site they are campaign photos, never shown as real customers or reviews.

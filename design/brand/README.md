# MASQ. brand boards

Two brand-identity boards the owner shared, written down here because the
pictures came through the chat only (not as files). Add the pictures to this
folder to keep them with the project.

They are kept as reference. `DESIGN.md` still rules the shop's look; where
these boards differ from it (noted at the end), the owner decides which wins.

## Board 1 — mark, pillars, palette, patterns, applications

- **Logo mark / icon:** the painted mask — split face (cream left with teal,
  orange right with a target eye), red mouth with bars, five cream strokes on
  top, purple and yellow brush marks, teal and yellow scribbles, pink dots.
- **Brand pillars** (each a round painted icon):
  - **Identity** (teal, an eye): we celebrate who we are, where we come from and where we're going.
  - **Heritage** (orange): rooted in African culture, symbols and ancestral wisdom.
  - **Creativity** (yellow): blending traditional influences with bold, modern expression.
  - **Future** (purple): building for a new generation that honours the past and creates what's next.
  - **Communication** (lime): speaking our truth, connecting with the world.
- **Colour palette:** `#0A0A0A` deep black · `#F5F3EC` bone white ·
  `#FF2E88` vibrant pink · `#FFC107` sun yellow · `#00C6B7` electric teal ·
  `#A6FF00` lime green · `#7B2DFF` royal purple · `#FF5A1F` burnt orange.
- **Pattern language:** eight hand-drawn line patterns, one in each colour.
- **Applications:** black tee, tote, hoodie, cap, gift box, swing tag, all with the mask.

## Board 2 — the full brand sheet

- **Line:** MASQ. — WE WEAR IDENTITY. "MASQ is a cultural streetwear brand
  that reimagines identity through design. Rooted in African heritage, symbols
  and futuristic thinking — we create pieces that speak beyond fashion."
  "THIS IS NOT WHAT YOU WEAR. IT'S WHO YOU ARE."
- **Logo suite:** primary logo (black brush MASQ), the mask icon.
- **Typography:** primary **Rift Bold**; secondary **Clash Grotesk**; accent
  handwritten ("Unapologetically you.").
- **Brand symbols:** Ori (head / destiny), Sankofa (go back and get it),
  Musubi (unity / connection), Nsibidi (communication), Future (progression).
- **The collections:**
  - **Ori** — premium line: minimal, spiritual, timeless.
  - **Egungun** — street core: bold, expressive, raw energy.
  - **Code** — symbolic line: layers of meaning, coded by culture.
  - **Neo Masq** — future / limited: reimagine, recode, redefine.
- **Packaging:** black pouch, swing tag, a "Thank you for wearing your
  identity" card, patterned tissue; inside each package a thank-you card, the
  meaning of the design, a brand sticker.
- **Brand info:** founded 2024 · Lagos, Nigeria · streetwear / cultural
  fashion · mission: to build a global movement that celebrates African
  identity through design, culture and storytelling.
- **Social and web:** Instagram, TikTok and Facebook @masq.world · X @masq_world ·
  masq.world · hello@masq.world. Closing line: "WHO ARE YOU WEARING?"

## Where these differ from the shop today (for the owner to decide)

- **Fonts.** The shop uses Inter and EB Garamond. The board names Rift Bold
  (a paid font from Fort Foundry) and Clash Grotesk (free from Fontshare).
  Switching needs a licence for Rift.
- **Purple.** The shop's violet is `#5a2be0`; the board's royal purple is `#7B2DFF`.
- **Collections.** The shop sells "Drop 01 — Urban Rituals" and "The
  Essentials". The board's four lines (Ori, Egungun, Code, Neo Masq) would need
  the collections pages the redesign plans.
- **Contact.** The shop's contact page says studio@masq.ng; the board says
  hello@masq.world and the masq.world domain.
- **Founded 2024.** The shop's footer says © 2026.

# MASQ. Film 01: "Worn, Not Spoken". The prompts

**Your picks, merged** ([the merged board](6-merged-selection.webp)):

- **All of board 1**: the storyboard, eight scenes, about 40 seconds.
- **Board 2, frame 5**: the man with the violet MASQ. wrap flowing round his body.
- **Board 4**: the masked figure in the raffia costume, walking down a dark corridor.
- **Board 5**: only the carved mask. No sneakers.

**How they fit together:**

- The masked figure (board 4) wears the carved mask (board 5). In Scene 04 he lifts the mask away, and the model's face is revealed ("Identity is not worn. It is revealed.").
- The flowing wrap (board 2, frame 5) becomes the scarf shot in Scene 05.
- The carved mask comes back at the end, just before the logo.

**The correct mask.** There are only two masks in the film:

- **Mask A, the official MASQ. mask** (the logo mark). It goes on every garment, print, emboss and graphic. Never an invented or "similar" mask.
- **Mask B, the carved mask** from board 5. It is the physical mask the figure wears.

Upload the matching pictures from [references/](references/) with every shot that shows a mask.

## Before you start

- **Google Flow runs Google's own model (Veo 3.1), not Seedance.** Seedance 2.5 is ByteDance's model, used on other sites (Dreamina, CapCut and similar). These prompts are written to work in both.
  - In **Flow**, each clip is about 8 seconds. Make the seven clips below one at a time, then join them in Flow's scene builder (or extend a clip from its last frame).
  - In **Seedance 2.5**, clips run up to about 30 seconds, so you can paste two or three clips together as one prompt.
- **Upload the reference pictures** with each shot. Flow calls this "Ingredients" and takes up to 3 pictures. Use the ones listed under each clip:
  - `A-official-masq-mask.png`
  - `B-carved-mask.png`
  - `C-masked-figure.png`
  - `D-the-flow.png`
- **Add the words in editing, not in the prompt.** Video models misspell text. The storyboard's lines ("We don't make clothes.", "We create symbols.", and so on) go on afterwards in CapCut, Flow or any editor, in the brand's brush lettering.
- **ChatGPT images:** paste the same clip prompt, then add this line at the end:

  > *Make one still image of the key moment of this shot, 16:9, photographic, no text.*

  Upload the same reference pictures to ChatGPT too.
- Always paste the **Style lock** first, then the clip. Paste the **Avoid** list wherever the site has a "negative prompt" box (in ChatGPT, add it at the end).

## Style lock (paste at the top of every clip)

```
STYLE LOCK — MASQ. FILM 01 "WORN, NOT SPOKEN"
Cinematic luxury fashion film for MASQ., a modern African streetwear brand. 16:9, 24 fps, shot on a full-frame cinema camera with anamorphic lenses, shallow depth of field, fine film grain. Every shot on deep black or very dark backgrounds. Lighting is directional and dramatic: a single hard key light from the side, deep shadows, no fill. Colour grade: rich crushed blacks with a slight blue-violet undertone; the only strong colours come from the mask artwork — teal #00C6B7, orange #FF5A1F, yellow #FFC107, violet #7B2DFF, pink #FF2E88, cream #F5F3EC. Movement is slow, deliberate and intentional — slow push-ins, slow orbits, slow motion fabric. Nothing bouncy, no fast cuts, no shaky camera. Mood: quiet power, heritage, confidence, ritual.

THE OFFICIAL MASQ. MASK (Mask A — use exactly this, see reference A): a vertical split-face African mask in a flat, hand-printed, rough-brush style. Left half teal with a white almond-shaped eye and a pink cheek; right half orange-red with a round "sun" eye (teal and orange rings with short white rays); a long cream-white nose ridge down the centre; an open oval red mouth with white vertical teeth bars; five short cream vertical strokes like a crown on top; a violet brush stroke top-left, yellow triangles, a violet dotted triangle, teal zigzag scribbles lower-left, a pink brush square lower-right, an orange dot grid upper-right. It is the only mask on any clothing, print, embroidery or emboss, always this exact design, never redrawn, never symmetrical, never a different tribal mask.

THE CARVED MASK (Mask B — see reference B): a real carved wooden ceremonial mask, long oval face, polished ebony-black wood with worn, chipped paint panels in teal, ochre-yellow and orange-red, violet and cobalt dotted patterns along the edges, deep hollow almond eyes, a long sharp ridge nose, a small open round mouth, glossy highlights on the wood.

THE MODEL: a young Black Nigerian man in his mid-twenties, dark skin, short twisted hair, strong jaw, calm and intense expression, the same man in every shot.

WARDROBE (all MASQ.): a royal-violet heavyweight cotton t-shirt with Mask A printed large on the chest and a small "MASQ." wordmark under it; a black satin bomber jacket with Mask A embroidered large on the back; a black cap with Mask A embossed tone-on-tone; a black leather backpack with Mask A embossed on the front; a violet silk wrap printed with Mask A and small brand symbols.

SOUND: no dialogue, no voice-over. Deep slow talking drum and low sub-bass pulses, soft fabric and leather foley, a breath of wind; rising gently towards the end.
```

## The seven clips

### Clip 1: Void and Material (Scenes 01–02 · 8 s)
References: **A**
```
[Paste STYLE LOCK]
SHOT: Opens on two seconds of pure black silence. Then a slow sequence of macro shots, each dissolving softly into the next: (1) black pebbled leather texture, the camera slides slowly sideways, light grazing across the grain; (2) extreme close-up of Mask A deeply embossed into black leather, slow push-in, the light revealing its edges; (3) royal-violet cotton fabric rippling slowly in darkness; (4) macro of black thread stitching along a leather seam, slow tilt up. Quiet, tactile, expensive. Leave dark empty space on the right of the frame for words added later.
```

### Clip 2: Symbol Formation (Scene 03 · 4 s)
References: **A**
```
[Paste STYLE LOCK]
SHOT: Against total black, painted fragments float and turn slowly in the air — shards of teal, orange, yellow, violet and pink with rough brushed edges and cream lines — as the camera slowly orbits. The fragments drift together and lock into place, assembling into Mask A exactly, flat and graphic, glowing softly. Slow push-in as the last pieces click together. Ends on Mask A complete, centred.
```

### Clip 3: The Reveal (Scene 04 + boards 4 and 5 · 8 s)
References: **B**, **C**, **A**
```
[Paste STYLE LOCK]
SHOT: A long, narrow, dark concrete corridor, a single cold light from the far end, soft haze. A tall masked figure walks slowly towards the camera: he wears Mask B, the carved wooden mask, and a long black raffia-fringe costume that sways as he moves, as in reference C. He stops in a shaft of side light, raises one hand to the mask and slowly lifts it away from his face. Cut to a medium close-up as the light fades up: it is the model, now in the royal-violet MASQ. t-shirt with Mask A on the chest, slow push-in. End on a close-up of his face, half-lit, half in shadow, eyes steady on the lens. The moment is calm and powerful: identity revealed.
```

### Clip 4: Product Details and The Flow (Scene 05 + board 2 frame 5 · 8 s)
References: **D**, **A**
```
[Paste STYLE LOCK]
SHOT: A sequence of slow product close-ups on black, each dissolving into the next: (1) micro pan across the Mask A print on the violet t-shirt, showing the texture of the ink on cotton; (2) the black cap turning slowly at 45 degrees, Mask A embossed tone-on-tone catching the light; (3) the black leather backpack, slow push-in, a hand slowly pulls the zip, Mask A embossed on the front. (4) Final beat, as in reference D: the model in black, wearing dark sunglasses, stands against black while the violet silk MASQ. wrap printed with Mask A billows and flows around his body and arms in slow motion, the fabric rippling like water and catching violet light.
```

### Clip 5: Movement (Scene 06 · 6 s)
References: **A**
```

Cut to behind him: he wears the black satin bomber with Mask A embroidered large on the back, the camera follows his walk. Finish on a slow-motion close-up of his hand brushing along the bomber's fabric, light sliding over the satin.

SHOT: Close-up of Mask A printed on the chest of the royal-violet t-shirt, the model breathing slowly, the print filling the frame. Dissolve to Mask A alone, centred on pure black, clean and flat, perfectly still, a faint glow around its colours. Leave space on the right for words added later.
```

### Clip 7: Final Frame (Scene 08 · 6 s)
References: **B**, **A**
```
[Paste STYLE LOCK]
SHOT: Mask B, the carved wooden mask, floats alone in total black, lit from the side, turning very slowly towards the camera; tiny flecks of teal, orange, violet and yellow paint drift around it like dust. It slowly dissolves into darkness, and Mask A appears small and centred, then settles to the left of the centre with clear black space to its right for the MASQ. wordmark and the line "ENTER THE WORLD OF MASQ." added later. Hold still for the last two seconds. Sound swells and falls to silence.
```

## Avoid (negative prompt)

```
text, letters, words, captions, subtitles, watermarks, extra logos, misspelled brand names, a different or redrawn mask, a symmetrical mask, generic tribal mask, cartoon or 3D-render look, plastic skin, distorted hands, extra fingers, warped faces, a different model between shots, bright or white backgrounds, daylight, colourful backgrounds, neon, sneakers, shoes in close-up, fast cuts, shaky camera, bouncy or comic motion, lens flares, oversaturated colours
```

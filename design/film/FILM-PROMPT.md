# MASQ. Film 01: "Worn, Not Spoken". The prompt

**One prompt** does both jobs: it makes the storyboard picture in ChatGPT and the film in Google Flow (or Seedance). Paste it as it is in both.

**What to attach, in this order:**
1. [The merged board](6-merged-selection.webp)
2. Dayo's test shot
3. Ifeoma's test shot
4. Kunle's test shot

(In Google Flow, "Ingredients" takes 3 pictures: attach the board, Dayo and Ifeoma.)

**The order to work in:**
1. Run it in ChatGPT first, to get the new storyboard picture.
2. Then run the same prompt in Google Flow. Attach the new storyboard picture from ChatGPT in place of the old board, so the film follows it.
3. If Flow stops early (it makes about 8 seconds at a time), press **Extend** and paste the prompt again.
4. Add the words and the logo in editing: video tools misspell text.

When the film is made, put its link in **admin → Settings → Film**. It then plays on the home page and in the Lookbook and Archive film panels.

```
MASQ. — FILM 01 "WORN, NOT SPOKEN"
Attached: 1. the merged MASQ. Film 01 board. 2. Dayo's test shot. 3. Ifeoma's test shot. 4. Kunle's test shot.

A cinematic luxury fashion film for MASQ., a Nigerian streetwear brand. Follow the storyboard and the pictures A, B, C and D on the attached board, but re-shoot everything fresh with our own models, a stronger film direction and a premium finish. The story: a hidden figure walks out of the dark, lifts off his mask and becomes himself; the MASQ. mask is the language.

THE CAST — keep each face exactly as in their test shot, in every frame:
— DAYO, the lead: 27, Yoruba, 1.88 m, athletic. Caramel-honey skin with golden undertones, high cheekbones, strong chiselled jaw, deep-set light brown eyes, short black 4C coils with a sharp shape-up and low fade, a neatly trimmed short beard.
— IFEOMA: 24, Igbo, 1.80 m, slim. Golden-honey skin with peach undertones, sculpted cheekbones, large almond light brown eyes, a short close-cropped natural 4C cut, small gold studs.
— KUNLE: 25, 1.86 m, lean. Deep ebony-brown skin with cool undertones, sculptural face, close-cropped hair, faint stubble, a small gold hoop in his left ear.
Real people, never CGI: true skin texture with visible pores, fine hairs, natural sheen on the cheekbones and nose bridge, small natural marks and asymmetry. No airbrushing, no smoothing, no plastic skin.

CAMERA AND LIGHT: shot on an ARRI Alexa 35 with Cooke anamorphic lenses (40 mm wides, 75 mm mediums, 135 mm macro details), T2.3, shallow depth of field, oval bokeh, fine 35 mm film grain. Low-key chiaroscuro: one hard side key light, black negative fill, a thin rim light separating figures from the dark, light haze so the beams show. Grade: crushed rich blacks with a blue-violet undertone, warm true skin tones; the only bright colours come from the masks: teal #00C6B7, orange #FF5A1F, yellow #FFC107, violet #7B2DFF, pink #FF2E88, cream #F5F3EC. Slow, deliberate camera: push-ins, orbits, tracking, slow-motion fabric, soft dissolves. No fast cuts, no shaky camera, nothing bouncy.

THE TWO MASKS:
— Mask A, the official MASQ. mask (picture A). Copy it exactly on every t-shirt, jacket, cap, backpack, wrap and graphic: the vertical split face, teal left half with a white almond eye, orange-red right half with a round sun-eye, a cream nose ridge, a red open mouth with white teeth bars, five cream strokes on top, and the violet, yellow, pink and teal brush marks around it. Never redraw it, never make it symmetrical, never use another mask.
— Mask B, the carved wooden mask (picture B): polished ebony wood with worn, chipped teal, ochre and orange-red paint and violet and cobalt dotted edges, hollow almond eyes, a long sharp nose ridge. Only in Scene 04 and Scene 08.

WARDROBE (all MASQ.): a royal-violet t-shirt with Mask A large on the chest; a black satin bomber with Mask A embroidered on the back; a black cap with Mask A embossed tone-on-tone; a black leather backpack with Mask A embossed; a violet silk wrap printed with Mask A.

THE FILM, IN ORDER (about 40 seconds):
SCENE 01 — VOID (0–2s): pure black, silence. Words: "WE DON'T MAKE CLOTHES."
SCENE 02 — MATERIAL LANGUAGE (2–8s): macro of black pebbled leather, a raking light across the grain; Mask A deeply embossed in black leather, the light catching its ridges; royal-violet cotton folding like a slow wave; black stitching on a leather seam, slow tilt up. Words: "WE CREATE SYMBOLS."
SCENE 03 — SYMBOL FORMATION (8–12s): painted fragments in the mask's colours suspended in black haze, lit like dust in a beam, slowly orbiting, then locking together into Mask A, glowing softly.
SCENE 04 — THE REVEAL (12–20s): low angle down a long wet concrete corridor, one cold light at the far end reflecting on the floor; a figure in a long black raffia-fringe costume wearing Mask B walks slowly towards camera (picture C). In a shaft of side light his hand lifts Mask B away: it is Dayo, in the royal-violet MASQ. t-shirt, rim-lit, the mask lowered in his hand. Slow push-in to an extreme close-up of his face, half in shadow, eyes into the lens. Words: "IDENTITY IS NOT WORN. IT IS REVEALED."
SCENE 05 — PRODUCT DETAILS (20–28s): macro across the Mask A print on violet cotton; the black cap turning at 45°, a rim light tracing the brim; Kunle's hand pulling the zip of the black leather backpack; then Ifeoma against black (picture D), the violet silk MASQ. wrap billowing around her body in slow motion, light catching the folds. Words: "ROOTED IN CULTURE."
SCENE 06 — MOVEMENT (28–34s): a wide shot of Dayo, Ifeoma and Kunle walking in a line through a single pool of hard light, long shadows; from behind, Kunle in the black satin bomber with Mask A on the back, haze and rim light; a slow-motion close-up of Ifeoma's hand trailing the edge of the wrap.
SCENE 07 — BRAND STATEMENT (34–37s): Mask A on Dayo's chest rising with his breath, dissolving to Mask A alone, centred on black. Words: "MASQ. IS A LANGUAGE. WORN. NOT SPOKEN."
SCENE 08 — FINAL FRAME (37–40s): Mask B floating in black, turning slowly towards camera, flecks of paint drifting around it; it dissolves into the MASQ. logo on black. Words: "ENTER THE WORLD OF MASQ."

SOUND (film only): no dialogue, no voice-over. A slow talking drum and low sub-bass pulses, soft fabric and leather sounds, a breath of wind, rising gently, then falling to silence.

OUTPUT:
— As a picture (ChatGPT): one storyboard sheet, landscape 3:2, largest size. Every frame a real campaign still, with its scene label, time code and a one-line camera note; the "Words" as brush-lettered text panels; the header "MASQ. | FILM 01 | WORN, NOT SPOKEN | STORYBOARD FRAMES | ~40s | 16:9 | 24 FPS | 4K"; the colour palette strip bottom right; black background, thin grey borders. Spell "MASQ." exactly, with the full stop.
— As a film (Google Flow / Seedance): one continuous 16:9 film at 24 fps, scene after scene as above, with sound. Show no words, letters or logos on screen except Mask A on the clothes: the words are added in editing.

AVOID: a different or redrawn mask, a symmetrical or generic tribal mask, changed faces, a different person between shots, cartoon, 3D-render or painted looks, plastic skin, extra fingers or warped hands, other brands or logos, misspelled "MASQ.", bright or daylight backgrounds, neon, sneakers or shoes in close-up, fast cuts, shaky camera, lens flares, oversaturated colour.
```

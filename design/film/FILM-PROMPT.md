# MASQ. Film 01: "Worn, Not Spoken". The prompt

Two prompts, one after the other: **the ChatGPT prompt** made the storyboard ([the finished storyboard, 21 Oct](8-storyboard-v3.png)); **the Google Flow prompt** at the end turns it into the film.

## 1. ChatGPT: the storyboard (done)

**What to attach, in this order:**
1. [The new storyboard](7-storyboard-v2.png) (made in ChatGPT, 21 Oct)
2. [The merged board](6-merged-selection.webp)
3. [Dayo](references/cast-dayo.png)
4. [Ifeoma](references/cast-ifeoma.png)
5. [Kunle](references/cast-kunle.png)
6. [The Face Tee print](references/E-face-tee.png)

(In Google Flow, "Ingredients" takes 3 pictures: attach the newest storyboard, Dayo and the Face Tee.)

**The order to work in:**
1. Run it in ChatGPT first, to get the new storyboard picture.
2. Then run the same prompt in Google Flow, attaching the newest storyboard ChatGPT gives you as image 1, so the film follows it.
3. If Flow stops early (it makes about 8 seconds at a time), press **Extend** and paste the prompt again.
4. Add the words and the logo in editing: video tools misspell text.

When the film is made, put its link in **admin → Settings → Film**. It then plays on the home page and in the Lookbook and Archive film panels.

```
MASQ. — FILM 01 "WORN, NOT SPOKEN"
Attached: 1. the new MASQ. Film 01 storyboard (the shot plan). 2. the merged board with reference pictures A, B, C and D. 3. Dayo's test shot. 4. Ifeoma's test shot. 5. Kunle's test shot. 6. the MASQ. Face Tee print.

A cinematic luxury fashion film for MASQ., a Nigerian streetwear brand. Follow the new storyboard (image 1) shot for shot: its scenes, framing, camera moves, mood and layout. Use the merged board (image 2) only for the reference pictures A, B, C and D. Re-shoot every frame fresh as a premium campaign still, with these corrections to image 1: the faces must become our three models exactly (images 3, 4, 5); every violet, black or white t-shirt must carry the full Face Tee print from image 6, with its ring of words; Ifeoma wears the black Face Tee, not a dress; and the time codes are the ones written below. The story: a hidden figure walks out of the dark, lifts off his mask and becomes himself; the MASQ. mask is the language.

THE CAST — the faces must be exactly the people in images 3, 4 and 5, recognisably the same in every frame; never blend or swap their faces:
— DAYO (image 3), the lead: 27, Yoruba, 1.88 m, athletic. Warm caramel-brown skin with golden undertones, light brown eyes, a strong chiselled jaw, full lips, short black curly 4C hair on top with a skin fade and a sharp line-up, a short full beard and moustache.
— IFEOMA (image 4): 24, Igbo, 1.80 m, slim. Warm golden-brown skin with natural freckles across the cheeks and nose, large almond brown eyes, high cheekbones, full lips, a very short natural cut of tight black 4C curls, small gold stud earrings.
— KUNLE (image 5): 25, 1.86 m, lean. Deep brown skin, a long lean face with high cheekbones, a broad nose, full lips, a thin moustache and light stubble along the jaw, close-cropped black hair with a sharp natural hairline, a small gold hoop earring, a long neck with a visible Adam's apple.
Real people, never CGI: true skin texture with visible pores, fine hairs, natural sheen on the cheekbones and nose bridge, small natural marks and asymmetry, as in their test shots. No airbrushing, no smoothing, no plastic skin.

THE FACE TEE (image 6) — the t-shirt every model wears where the film says "Face Tee": a heavyweight cotton crew-neck t-shirt with one large chest print, copied exactly from image 6: Mask A in the centre, inside a ring of white text — "WE WEAR IDENTITY •" arcing over the top and "UNAPOLOGETICALLY YOURS" arcing under the bottom — with small coloured dashes on the left and right of the ring; below the ring the "MASQ." wordmark in brush letters (M cream, A yellow, S teal, Q orange-red, with the full stop); and under it a row of five small symbols divided by thin bars: a teal diamond, an orange-red cross-star, a yellow ring, a violet star and a green target. Every letter spelled exactly as written; the print sits flat on the chest and follows the fabric's folds.
Who wears it: Dayo in the royal-violet Face Tee; Ifeoma in the black Face Tee; Kunle in the white Face Tee under the open black satin bomber.

CAMERA AND LIGHT: shot on an ARRI Alexa 35 with Cooke anamorphic lenses (40 mm wides, 75 mm mediums, 135 mm macro details), T2.3, shallow depth of field, oval bokeh, fine 35 mm film grain. Low-key chiaroscuro: one hard side key light, black negative fill, a thin rim light separating figures from the dark, light haze so the beams show. Grade: crushed rich blacks with a blue-violet undertone, warm true skin tones; the only bright colours come from the masks and prints: teal #00C6B7, orange #FF5A1F, yellow #FFC107, violet #7B2DFF, pink #FF2E88, cream #F5F3EC. Slow, deliberate camera: push-ins, orbits, tracking, slow-motion fabric, soft dissolves. No fast cuts, no shaky camera, nothing bouncy.

THE TWO MASKS:
— Mask A, the official MASQ. mask (picture A on the merged board, and the centre of the Face Tee print). Copy it exactly on every t-shirt, jacket, cap, backpack, wrap and graphic: the vertical split face, teal left half with a white almond eye, orange-red right half with a round sun-eye, a cream nose ridge, a red open mouth with white teeth bars, five cream strokes on top, and the violet, yellow, pink and teal brush marks around it. Never redraw it, never make it symmetrical, never use another mask.
— Mask B, the carved wooden mask (picture B): polished ebony wood with worn, chipped teal, ochre and orange-red paint and violet and cobalt dotted edges, hollow almond eyes, a long sharp nose ridge. Only in Scene 04 and Scene 08.

OTHER WARDROBE (all MASQ.): a black satin bomber with Mask A embroidered large on the back; a black cap with Mask A embossed tone-on-tone; a black leather backpack with Mask A embossed; a violet silk wrap printed with Mask A.

THE FILM, IN ORDER (about 40 seconds):
SCENE 01 — VOID (0–2s): pure black, silence. Words: "WE DON'T MAKE CLOTHES."
SCENE 02 — MATERIAL LANGUAGE (2–8s): macro of black pebbled leather, a raking light across the grain; Mask A deeply embossed in black leather, the light catching its ridges; royal-violet cotton folding like a slow wave; black stitching on a leather seam, slow tilt up. Words: "WE CREATE SYMBOLS."
SCENE 03 — SYMBOL FORMATION (8–12s): painted fragments in the mask's colours suspended in black haze, lit like dust in a beam, slowly orbiting, then locking together into Mask A, glowing softly.
SCENE 04 — THE REVEAL (12–20s): low angle down a long wet concrete corridor, one cold light at the far end reflecting on the floor; a figure in a long black raffia-fringe costume wearing Mask B walks slowly towards camera (picture C). In a shaft of side light his hand lifts Mask B away: it is Dayo, now wearing the royal-violet Face Tee, the whole chest print with its ring of words sharp and readable, rim-lit, the mask lowered in his hand. Slow push-in to an extreme close-up of his face, half in shadow, eyes into the lens. Words: "IDENTITY IS NOT WORN. IT IS REVEALED."
SCENE 05 — PRODUCT DETAILS (20–28s): a slow macro pan around the ring of words on Dayo's violet Face Tee — "WE WEAR IDENTITY •" then "UNAPOLOGETICALLY YOURS" — ending on Mask A and the "MASQ." wordmark, the ink texture visible on the cotton; the black cap turning at 45°, a rim light tracing the brim; Kunle's hand pulling the zip of the black leather backpack; then Ifeoma in the black Face Tee against black (picture D), the violet silk MASQ. wrap billowing around her body in slow motion, opening to show the Face Tee print beneath, light catching the folds. Words: "ROOTED IN CULTURE."
SCENE 06 — MOVEMENT (28–34s): a wide shot of Dayo (violet Face Tee), Ifeoma (black Face Tee) and Kunle (white Face Tee under the open black bomber) walking side by side through a single pool of hard light, long shadows, all three chest prints catching the light; from behind, Kunle's black satin bomber with Mask A embroidered on the back, haze and rim light; a slow-motion close-up of Ifeoma's hand trailing the edge of the wrap.
SCENE 07 — BRAND STATEMENT (34–37s): a close-up of the Face Tee print on Dayo's chest, the ring of words and Mask A rising with his breath, dissolving to Mask A alone, centred on black. Words: "MASQ. IS A LANGUAGE. WORN. NOT SPOKEN."
SCENE 08 — FINAL FRAME (37–40s): Mask B floating in black, turning slowly towards camera, flecks of paint drifting around it; it dissolves into the MASQ. logo on black. Words: "ENTER THE WORLD OF MASQ."

SOUND (film only): no dialogue, no voice-over. A slow talking drum and low sub-bass pulses, soft fabric and leather sounds, a breath of wind, rising gently, then falling to silence.

OUTPUT:
— As a picture (ChatGPT): one storyboard sheet, landscape 3:2, largest size. Every frame a real campaign still, with its scene label, time code and a one-line camera note; the "Words" as brush-lettered text panels; the header "MASQ. | FILM 01 | WORN, NOT SPOKEN | STORYBOARD FRAMES | ~40s | 16:9 | 24 FPS | 4K"; the colour palette strip bottom right; black background, thin grey borders. Spell "MASQ." exactly, with the full stop.
— As a film (Google Flow / Seedance): one continuous 16:9 film at 24 fps, scene after scene as above, with sound. Show no words on screen except the Face Tee print and Mask A on the clothes: the other words are added in editing.

AVOID: a different or redrawn mask, a symmetrical or generic tribal mask, a misspelled or changed Face Tee print, missing ring of words, changed faces, a different person between shots, blended faces, cartoon, 3D-render or painted looks, plastic skin, extra fingers or warped hands, other brands or logos, misspelled "MASQ.", bright or daylight backgrounds, neon, sneakers or shoes in close-up, fast cuts, shaky camera, lens flares, oversaturated colour.
```

## 2. Google Flow: the film

Made from [the final storyboard](10-storyboard-final.webp) (21 Oct, 48 seconds). Flow (Veo 3.1) makes about 8 seconds at a time, so the film is **six parts with the same prompt**: only the second line changes, `MAKE NOW: PART 1` up to `PART 6`.

# Confetti chime: the choices

The sound that plays with the confetti (an order placed, a gift card ready).
**Chosen 21 Oct: 15. Confetti poof**, played with the confetti when an order is placed. 1. Sparkle stays for the other big moments (a gift card ready, a password reset).
Made by `tools/make-sounds.py` (1–2) and `tools/make-sound-options.py` (3–8).

| # | Name | What it sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sparkle (in the shop: gift card ready, password reset) | A warm chord swelling in, high notes twinkling down like the confetti falling |
| 2 | Warm bells | Three slow, low bells; the calmest |
| 3 | Kalimba | An African thumb piano playing a bright five-note rise; ties to the brand's roots |
| 4 | Harp | A soft upward sweep of plucked strings, settling on a chord; elegant |
| 5 | Music box | A delicate little high tune; sweet and gift-like |
| 6 | Wind chimes | Crystal tubes in a breeze, airy and slow; the most "soothing" |
| 7 | Talking drum + bell | A soft drum note bending down, then a bright bell; the most Nigerian |
| 8 | Soft pop | A modern app "done": two warm notes up with a gentle thump; the shortest |

## The softer set

Quieter (about a quarter lower at their loudest), lower notes, no sharp start, the bright edges filtered off, and more room around them.

| 12 | Soft kalimba | The thumb piano an octave lower, muted with a thumb |
| 13 | Gentle chimes | A few wind chimes, slow and far away |

## Confetti itself

Built from the sound confetti really makes: a soft "poof" of air as it bursts, then the light papery rustle of the pieces fluttering down, thinning out over about two and a half seconds. In stereo, with one burst on the left and one on the right, as the confetti fires from both sides on screen (best heard on headphones or a laptop).

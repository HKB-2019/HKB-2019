# Klae

Klae lives here, in its own folder, next to the MASQ. shop but separate from it. Nothing in `klae/` is part of the shop, its build or its tests.

## Where it began: the dream

Earlier this year, the founder had a dream.

> I saw myself walking with a group of friends. It seemed we were going to attend a friend's (or some friends') graduation ceremony, in an academic environment.
>
> I asked a question to find out where the friends were going to celebrate. Then someone walked up to me and flipped out a **debit card**. I looked at the debit card and saw a square face buffering, as if an app was about to open. Then a screen on that same card showed me the venue of the graduation ceremony, and I could see the friend I was looking for.
>
> I said, "Wow, I didn't know this was possible." I said it two or three times, and then I woke up.

## What the dream tells us

- **It is a debit card.** Not a generic card, not a phone, not a badge: a debit card, the thing tied to a person's money and bank account, carried every day.
- **The card wakes up.** A square face appears and buffers, like an app opening. The card has a screen and something live behind it.
- **A question gets answered.** The dreamer asked *where are they celebrating?* and the card answered by showing the place.
- **It shows a place and a person.** The screen showed the venue *and* the friend in it: where, and who is there, live.
- **Someone else's card helped.** A person walked up and showed their card. Klae is useful to the people around its holder, not only to the holder.
- **The feeling.** "Wow, I didn't know this was possible," said more than once. That is the reaction Klae is for.

## Open questions

Still to be decided, together, before anything is built:

1. Is Klae a physical debit card with its own screen, a card experience on a phone, or both?
2. How does the card know the venue: through a payment, a ticket or invitation bought with it, a shared event, or something else?
3. What does "seeing the friend" mean: their presence at the venue, a photo, a live view, a message?
4. Who opens what: what does the holder choose to show, and what stays private?
5. Who is Klae for first: students and graduates, friends and celebrations, or everyone who carries a debit card?

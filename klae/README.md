# Klae

Klae lives here, in its own folder, next to the MASQ. shop but separate from it. Nothing in `klae/` is part of the shop, its build or its tests.

## Where it began: the dream

Earlier this year, the founder had a dream.

> I saw myself walking with a group of friends. It seemed we were going to attend a friend's (or some friends') graduation ceremony, in an academic environment.
>
> I asked a question to find out where the friends were going to celebrate. Then someone walked up to me and flipped out a **debit card**. I looked at the debit card and saw a square face buffering, as if an app was about to open. Then a screen on that same card showed me the venue of the graduation ceremony, and I could see the friend I was looking for.
>
> I said, "Wow, I didn't know this was possible." I said it two or three times, and then I woke up.

## Direction

**Klae is a premier AMOLED debit card for business men and women:** send and receive money, make video calls, send short mail and handle business from the card itself. The full vision is in [`VISION.md`](VISION.md).

In the dream, the card's square face buffering and its screen showing a place and a person are what the Premier card does: an AMOLED screen that wakes, and a video call live on the card.

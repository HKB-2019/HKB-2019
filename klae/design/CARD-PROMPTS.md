# Prompts to design the Klae Premier card

For ChatGPT. **Send the messages in the same chat, in order**, and wait for each picture before sending the next. Messages 1 to 3 decide the look; 4 to 10 design what the screen shows; 11 and 12 show the card in real life.

**Before message 2, choose** a wordmark from message 1 (A, B or C). **Before message 3, choose** a direction from message 2, and replace `[DIRECTION]` and `[WORDMARK]` in messages 3 to 12 with your choices.

**Every time:**
- Ask for the largest size and download the full file, never a screenshot.
- Check every word on the card is spelt right, **Klae** especially. ChatGPT often garbles small text: if it does, reply *"Keep everything, but fix the text: it must read exactly: …"*.
- Don't ask for real bank or card-network logos (Visa, Mastercard, Verve) or real bank names: ChatGPT may refuse, and Klae can't use them before a partner agrees. The prompts use a plain placeholder.
- The people in messages 8 and 11 are AI-made: use the pictures as concept designs, never as real customers.

**Quick fixes, as a reply in the same chat:**
- Looks cheap or toy-like: *"More premium: real brushed metal with fine grain, crisp machined edges, deeper blacks on the screen, softer, more controlled studio light. Less shine, less glow."*
- Too busy: *"Simpler: remove anything that isn't needed, more empty space, one accent colour only."*
- Card changed between pictures: *"Keep the card exactly as in picture 3: same metal, colour, wordmark and layout."*
- Doesn't look like a real photograph: *"Make it a real product photograph: true-to-life materials, natural reflections, slight film grain, never a 3D render."*

## 1. The brief and the wordmark

```
I am designing a new product and want you as my art director for this whole chat. Keep everything we decide consistent in every image I ask for here.

The product: the Klae Premier card, by Klae. A premium metal debit card for business men and women, with a living AMOLED screen built into its face. From the card itself you can send and receive money, make video calls, read and send short mail, and handle business: invoices, approvals, receipts. It is for founders, executives and traders in Nigeria and the diaspora who run their work on the move. It should feel like the most expensive object in their wallet: quiet, precise, confident. Think fine watches and private banking, not gadgets or crypto.

The name is Klae (capital K, then lowercase l, a, e). First, show me three wordmark options for Klae, side by side on a plain off-white background, in black, labelled A, B and C underneath:
A: a refined high-contrast serif, elegant and calm.
B: a precise geometric sans-serif, wide letter spacing.
C: a custom wordmark where the K has one small distinctive detail, minimal and memorable.
No icons, no taglines, no gradients. The name must be spelt exactly Klae.
```

## 2. Three directions for the card

```
I choose wordmark [WORDMARK]. Use it on the card from now on.

Now show me three design directions for the Klae Premier card, side by side, front faces only, lying flat on a dark stone surface, soft overhead studio light, photographed straight down. Each is a standard bank-card shape (85.6 × 54 mm, rounded corners). Label them 1, 2 and 3 underneath.

All three have, on the front: the Klae wordmark, a gold-coloured EMV chip, a small contactless symbol, a small fingerprint sensor (a slim oval), and a small plain rectangle reading "NETWORK" in the bottom-right corner as a placeholder for the card-network logo. No card number on the front: it shows on the screen only when asked.

1. "The square face": obsidian-black brushed metal, with a square AMOLED screen set into the face (about 22 mm square, sharp black glass, lit with a soft clock and the Klae wordmark). A square face is how the card first appeared to me in a dream.
2. "Edge to edge": the whole front is one black AMOLED glass screen in a thin champagne-gold metal frame, showing a calm dark home screen with the time and the Klae wordmark.
3. "Titanium": raw grey titanium with a fine brushed grain, and a slim horizontal AMOLED strip screen across the upper third, showing the time and a balance hidden behind dots.

Real product photograph, never a 3D render. Every word spelt exactly as I wrote it.
```

## 5. Waking up: the square face

```
This is the most important picture. In my dream, someone flipped out this card, and on its face a square appeared, buffering as if an app was about to open.

Show the card in a hand at night, close up, in a calm dark room. The screen is waking up: true black, and in its centre a small glowing square slowly forming, drawn in a thin gold line, partly complete, as if loading. Nothing else on the screen. The light from the screen touches the fingertips a little. Real photograph, shallow depth of field, slight film grain.
```

## The card

Every Klae Premier card has the same two features:

- **Edge to edge:** the whole front is one black AMOLED glass screen in a thin metal frame. Only the chip sits in a small window in the glass, in its usual place; the fingerprint is read through the screen, at a marked spot.
- **The square face:** the card's own loading symbol. Whenever the card wakes or opens something, a small square forms in the centre of the screen and buffers, just as in the dream.

The **type** is the metal: it shows on the frame around the screen, the edges and the whole back.

## Card types to choose from

| Type | The metal | Feels like |
|---|---|---|
| **Titanium** | Raw grey titanium, fine brushed grain | Engineered, light, strong |
| **Gold** | Warm champagne gold, satin | Classic wealth, warm |

| **Obsidian** | Deep matte black | Quiet power, private banking |
| **Graphite** | Dark grey, satin | Understated, technical |
| **Pearl** | White ceramic with a soft sheen | Light, calm, unusual |
| **Carbon** | Woven black carbon fibre | Fast, sporty, precise |
| **Bronze** | Dark warm bronze, brushed | Earthy, heritage |
| **Midnight** | Deep blue, anodised | Calm, trustworthy |
| **Emerald** | Deep green, anodised | Growth, money, a nod to Nigeria's green |

A suggested range of four, from first card to the top: **Titanium → Gold → Platinum → Obsidian** (by invitation only).

Avoid names other cards already own: Black, Infinite, Signature, World Elite, Centurion, Reserve and Sapphire.

**Every time:**
- Ask for the largest size and download the full file, never a screenshot.
- Check every word on the card is spelt right, **Klae** especially. ChatGPT often garbles small text: if it does, reply *"Keep everything, but fix the text: it must read exactly: …"*.
- Don't ask for real bank or card-network logos (Visa, Mastercard, Verve) or real bank names: ChatGPT may refuse, and Klae can't use them before a partner agrees. The prompts use a plain placeholder.
- The people in messages 8 and 11 are AI-made: use the pictures as concept designs, never as real customers.

**Quick fixes, as a reply in the same chat:**
- Looks cheap or toy-like: *"More premium: real brushed metal with fine grain, crisp machined edges, deeper blacks on the screen, softer, more controlled studio light. Less shine, less glow."*
- Too busy: *"Simpler: remove anything that isn't needed, more empty space, one accent colour only."*
- Card changed between pictures: *"Keep the card exactly as in picture 3: same metal, colour, wordmark and layout."*
- Doesn't look like a real photograph: *"Make it a real product photograph: true-to-life materials, natural reflections, slight film grain, never a 3D render."*

## 1. The brief and the wordmark

```
I am designing a new product and want you as my art director for this whole chat. Keep everything we decide consistent in every image I ask for here.

The product: the Klae Premier card, by Klae. A premium metal debit card for business men and women, with a living AMOLED screen built into its face. From the card itself you can send and receive money, make video calls, read and send short mail, and handle business: invoices, approvals, receipts. It is for founders, executives and traders in Nigeria and the diaspora who run their work on the move. It should feel like the most expensive object in their wallet: quiet, precise, confident. Think fine watches and private banking, not gadgets or crypto.

The name is Klae (capital K, then lowercase l, a, e). First, show me three wordmark options for Klae, side by side on a plain off-white background, in black, labelled A, B and C underneath:
A: a refined high-contrast serif, elegant and calm.
B: a precise geometric sans-serif, wide letter spacing.
C: a custom wordmark where the K has one small distinctive detail, minimal and memorable.
No icons, no taglines, no gradients. The name must be spelt exactly Klae.
```
